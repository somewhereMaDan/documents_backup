The replica is essentially saying:
"Give me the changes after position X."

Primary WAL:

A ─ B ─ C ─ D ─ E ─ F ─ G
                    ↑
                 replica
                 is here
                 
The primary sends:

E → F → G

The replica replays them.

Then it is caught up.

So why does the screenshot show 41 on replicas?

Your screenshot is demonstrating replication lag.

Initially:

PRIMARY:   likes = 41
REPLICA 1: likes = 41
REPLICA 2: likes = 41
REPLICA 3: likes = 41

For a short period you might have:

PRIMARY:   42
REPLICA 1: 41
REPLICA 2: 41
REPLICA 3: 41

Then replication catches up:

PRIMARY:   42
REPLICA 1: 42
REPLICA 2: 42
REPLICA 3: 42

That delay is called replication lag it depends on the system/load/network/configuration.
